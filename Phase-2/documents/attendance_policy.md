# Brightpeak Academy — Attendance Policy
**Document ID:** BP-ATT-2025-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-09-15  
**Applies to:** All enrolled students, all courses  
**Owner:** Academic Affairs Office

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the minimum attendance requirements for remaining eligible for final assessments, certificates, and progression decisions at Brightpeak Academy.

## 2. Minimum Attendance Threshold
Students must maintain **at least 75% attendance** in each individual course. Attendance is calculated as:

```
Attendance % = (Number of sessions attended / Total scheduled sessions) × 100
```

Falling below 75% may result in:
- A formal attendance warning issued by the instructor or registrar
- Temporary suspension of access to final assessments for that course
- In repeated cases, a recommendation for academic probation

## 3. Recording Attendance
- Instructors must record attendance for every scheduled session within 24 hours.
- Students may request a correction within 5 working days of the session date.
- Remote / hybrid sessions count equally with in-person sessions provided the student was logged in and participated for the majority of the session.

## 4. Excused Absences
The following absences may be marked as excused (and therefore do not reduce the attendance percentage) when documented:
- Medical emergency with a medical certificate
- Official university or academy representation
- Death of an immediate family member
- Court summons or mandatory government appointment

Excused absences must be submitted to the registrar within 7 days of the absence.

## 5. Warning and Escalation Process
1. When a student’s attendance drops below 80%, the system flags the enrollment and the instructor receives a soft notification.
2. When attendance drops below 75%, a formal warning is generated and the student is notified.
3. If the student does not recover above 75% by the final two weeks of the course, the student becomes ineligible for the final assessment unless an academic appeal is approved.

## 6. Appeals
Appeals against an attendance-related ineligibility decision must be submitted in writing to the Academic Affairs Office within 10 working days of the notification. Supporting evidence is required.

## 7. Relationship to Other Policies
- Students who withdraw under the Course Withdrawal Policy within the first 14 days are not subject to attendance sanctions for that course.
- Attendance data is one of the inputs considered under the Scholarship Policy when determining ongoing eligibility.
