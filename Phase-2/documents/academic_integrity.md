# Brightpeak Academy — Academic Integrity Rules
**Document ID:** BP-AI-2025-03  
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-08-20  
**Applies to:** All students, instructors, and staff  
**Owner:** Academic Integrity Committee

## 1. Purpose
Brightpeak Academy expects every member of its community to act with honesty, fairness, and respect for the work of others. This document defines prohibited conduct, investigation procedures, and sanctions.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct
The following actions constitute academic integrity violations:

### 2.1 Plagiarism
Presenting another person’s words, ideas, code, data, or creative work as one’s own without proper attribution. This includes:
- Copying text or code from the internet, classmates, or previous submissions without citation
- Paraphrasing substantial portions of a source without acknowledgement
- Submitting work generated by generative AI tools without explicit disclosure when the assignment requires original student work

### 2.2 Cheating
- Using unauthorized materials or devices during an examination or timed assessment
- Communicating with other students during an examination
- Obtaining or distributing examination questions or answers in advance

### 2.3 Unauthorized Collaboration
Working with other students on an assignment that has been designated as individual work, unless the instructor has explicitly permitted collaboration.

### 2.4 Fabrication
Inventing or falsifying data, citations, attendance records, or any academic record.

## 3. Investigation Process
1. An instructor or staff member who suspects a violation files a report with the Academic Integrity Committee within 5 working days of discovery.
2. The student is notified in writing and given 7 days to respond.
3. The Committee reviews the evidence and the student’s response. A hearing may be held if the facts are contested.
4. A decision is issued within 14 days of the hearing (or of the response deadline if no hearing is required).

## 4. Sanctions
Sanctions are applied according to severity and prior history:

| Severity | Typical Sanction |
|----------|------------------|
| First minor offence (e.g. missing citation) | Warning + mandatory integrity workshop |
| First major offence (plagiarism or cheating) | Zero on the assignment + academic probation for one term |
| Repeated or severe offence | Course failure, extended probation, or suspension |

Sanctions are recorded in the student’s permanent academic record and may affect scholarship eligibility and future recommendations.

## 5. Relationship to Grades and Scholarships
A confirmed academic integrity violation automatically renders the student ineligible for any merit scholarship for the remainder of the academic year. Grade averages used for scholarship calculations exclude any assignment that received a zero as a result of an integrity violation.

## 6. Appeals
A student may appeal a finding of violation or the severity of a sanction within 10 working days of the decision letter. Appeals are heard by a panel that excludes the original decision-makers.
