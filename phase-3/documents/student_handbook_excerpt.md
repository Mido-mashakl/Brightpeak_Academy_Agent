## Section 3 — Academic Tracks and Progression

Brightpeak Academy currently offers three primary academic tracks:

1. **AI & Machine Learning Track**  
   Core courses: Introduction to Machine Learning, Deep Learning Foundations, Applied NLP, MLOps Essentials.  
   Recommended prior level: Intermediate or Advanced.  
   Capstone: a production-ready model deployment project.

2. **Full-Stack Web Development Track**  
   Core courses: Frontend Fundamentals, Backend with Node & Python, Databases & APIs, DevOps for Web.  
   Recommended prior level: Beginner or Intermediate.  
   Capstone: a full-stack application with authentication and deployment.

3. **Data Engineering Track**  
   Core courses: Data Modeling, ETL Pipelines, Cloud Data Warehouses, Streaming Systems.  
   Recommended prior level: Intermediate.  
   Capstone: an end-to-end data pipeline with monitoring.

Students may change tracks once per academic year by submitting a track-change request to their academic advisor. A change after the midpoint of a term may require withdrawal from current courses under the Course Withdrawal Policy.

## Section 5 — Academic Advising and Support

Every student is assigned an academic advisor. Advisors review:
- Current enrollments and progress percentages
- Attendance trends
- Grade averages relative to scholarship thresholds
- Any active integrity or probation flags

Students flagged for attendance risk (below 80%) or scholarship risk (average approaching 90% from above or below) receive proactive outreach. The advisory system may generate personalised notes using the student’s raw academic data.

## Section 12 — Glossary of Key Thresholds

- **Attendance floor**: 75% per course (Attendance Policy)
- **Scholarship entry average**: > 90% across completed courses (Scholarship Policy)
- **Scholarship maintenance average**: ≥ 88% (Scholarship Policy)
- **Early withdrawal window**: 14 calendar days (Course Withdrawal Policy)
- **Late submission limit**: 3 days with progressive 10% daily penalty (Late Submission Policy)
- **Integrity first major offence**: zero on assignment + one-term probation (Academic Integrity Rules)

These thresholds are enforced by the academic system and are the authoritative numbers used by both staff tools and the AI assistant.
