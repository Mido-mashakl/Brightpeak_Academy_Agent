# Brightpeak Academy — Late Submission Policy
**Document ID:** BP-LS-2025-04  
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-07-10  
**Applies to:** All graded assignments and coursework  
**Owner:** Academic Affairs Office

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets transparent, consistent rules for the acceptance and scoring of work submitted after the published deadline.

## 2. Standard Deduction Schedule
Unless an instructor has published a course-specific exception in the syllabus:

- **Day 1 late** (submitted within 24 hours after the deadline): **10%** deduction from the maximum possible score.
- **Day 2 late**: **20%** deduction.
- **Day 3 late**: **30%** deduction.
- **After 3 full days**: the submission is **no longer accepted** and receives a score of zero.

The clock starts at the exact published deadline (including time zone). Partial days are rounded up to the next full day for deduction purposes.

## 3. Technical Failures
Students are responsible for verifying successful upload. A claim of technical failure must be accompanied by:
- Screenshot or system log showing the error, **and**
- Evidence that the work existed in substantially complete form before the deadline (e.g. local file timestamps, version-control history).

The registrar reviews technical-failure claims within 3 working days. If accepted, the late penalty may be waived or reduced.

## 4. Extensions
An extension may be granted **before** the original deadline when:
- A documented medical or family emergency exists, **or**
- The student has an approved disability accommodation that requires extra time.

Extensions are granted by the instructor (or the registrar if the instructor is unavailable) and must be recorded in the system. An approved extension resets the deadline; the late-submission clock then runs from the new deadline.

## 5. Group Assignments
For group work, the late penalty is applied to the whole group’s submission. Individual members who can demonstrate that they completed and submitted their portion on time may petition for individual relief; the petition is decided by the instructor.

## 6. Interaction with Other Policies
- Work that receives a late penalty remains eligible for academic-integrity review.
- A zero resulting from a submission more than 3 days late counts toward the overall average used by the Scholarship Policy.
- Students who withdraw under the Course Withdrawal Policy before the assignment deadline are not required to submit the work.
