# Brightpeak Academy — Merit Scholarship Policy
**Document ID:** BP-SCH-2025-02  
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-10-01  
**Applies to:** All students who have completed at least one full course  
**Owner:** Student Finance & Academic Affairs

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the criteria, application process, and conditions for the Brightpeak Academy Merit Scholarship, which covers up to 50% of tuition fees for eligible students.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
A student is eligible to apply when **all** of the following are true:

1. Overall grade average across all **completed** courses is **above 90%**.
2. The student has no active academic integrity violations on record.
3. The student is not currently on academic probation.
4. Attendance in every currently active course is at or above 75%.

Partial completion of a course does not count toward the average until the course status is marked “completed”.

## 3. Scholarship Coverage
- Up to **50% of tuition fees** for the subsequent academic term.
- The scholarship does not cover external examination fees, materials, or housing.
- Awards are granted for one term at a time and must be re-evaluated each term.

## 4. Application Process
1. The student submits an application via the student portal between the published open and close dates.
2. The Academic Affairs Office verifies the grade average, attendance, and integrity record.
3. A shortlist is produced and reviewed by the Scholarship Committee.
4. Successful applicants receive a formal award letter stating the percentage awarded and the term covered.

## 5. Maintenance Requirements
To retain the scholarship in subsequent terms the student must:
- Maintain an overall average of at least 88% across newly completed courses.
- Keep attendance at or above 75% in all active courses.
- Remain free of academic integrity violations.

Failure to meet any maintenance requirement results in the scholarship being suspended for the following term. Reinstatement requires a new application.

## 6. Interaction with Grade Changes
If a grade is later revised (upward or downward) and the revision moves the student across the 90% threshold, the eligibility status is recalculated. A downward revision that drops the average below 90% after an award has been issued does not automatically claw back the current-term award, but it will affect the next term’s eligibility.

## 7. Appeals
Students who believe an eligibility decision was made in error may appeal in writing within 14 days of the decision letter. Appeals are reviewed by a panel that did not participate in the original decision.
