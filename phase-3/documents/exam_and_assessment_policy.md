# Brightpeak Academy — Examination and Assessment Policy
**Document ID:** BP-EX-2025-06  
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-09-01  
**Applies to:** All graded assessments, mid-terms, and final examinations  
**Owner:** Academic Affairs Office & Examination Board

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the rules governing the design, administration, grading, and review of all formal assessments at Brightpeak Academy.

## 2. Types of Assessment
- **Formative assessments** (quizzes, in-class exercises) — may be optional or low-stakes; late-submission rules may be relaxed at the instructor’s discretion.
- **Summative assignments** — subject to the Late Submission Policy.
- **Mid-term and final examinations** — timed, invigilated or proctored assessments. No late submission is permitted once the examination window closes.

## 3. Eligibility to Sit Final Assessments
A student is eligible to sit the final examination or final project of a course only if:
1. Attendance is at or above 75% (see Attendance Policy), **and**
2. All mandatory coursework with a weight greater than 10% has been submitted (even if late and penalised), **and**
3. The student is not under an active academic-integrity suspension that bars examination sitting.

Students who become ineligible are notified in writing at least 7 days before the examination date where practicable.

## 4. Examination Conduct
- Students must present a valid Brightpeak student ID.
- Unauthorized materials, devices, or communication are prohibited (see Academic Integrity Rules).
- Any student found in possession of unauthorized material will have the paper confiscated and the incident referred to the Academic Integrity Committee.
- For online proctored examinations, the student must maintain a continuous camera feed and share their screen as required by the proctoring protocol.

## 5. Grading and Feedback
- Instructors must publish grades for summative assessments within 14 calendar days of the submission or examination date.
- Feedback must indicate the main strengths and areas for improvement; a numerical score alone is insufficient for assignments carrying more than 20% of the course grade.
- Grade changes after publication require a written justification and approval by the course lead or registrar when the change exceeds 15 points or crosses a scholarship threshold (90%).

## 6. Re-Assessment and Resit
- A student who fails a final assessment may be offered one resit opportunity, scheduled within 30 days of the original result release, provided attendance and integrity conditions are still met.
- The resit mark is capped at the bare pass mark (usually 50% or the course-specific pass threshold) unless the Examination Board grants an exception for documented mitigating circumstances.
- Coursework resubmission after a fail is at the instructor’s discretion and must be completed within the published resit window.

## 7. Mitigating Circumstances
Students who experience serious illness, bereavement, or other documented emergencies that affect an examination may apply for mitigating circumstances within 5 working days of the examination date. Supporting evidence is required. If accepted, the Examination Board may:
- Grant a deferred sitting without penalty, **or**
- Allow the existing coursework average to stand as the course result (in exceptional cases only).

## 8. Protocol 4.2b — Special Arrangements for Students with Documented Needs
Students with an approved disability or medical condition may request:
- Extra time (typically 25% or 50% additional)
- A separate examination room
- Assistive technology or a scribe
- Rest breaks

Requests must be submitted to Student Support at least 14 days before the examination. Protocol 4.2b decisions are recorded against the student’s enrollment and are visible to the invigilator.
