# Brightpeak Academy — Course Withdrawal Policy
**Document ID:** BP-WD-2025-05  
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-06-01  
**Applies to:** All enrolled students  
**Owner:** Registrar’s Office

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the conditions under which a student may withdraw from a course, the academic consequences of withdrawal, and the administrative process that must be followed.

## 2. Withdrawal Windows

### 2.1 Early Withdrawal (No Academic Penalty)
A student may withdraw from a course **within the first 14 calendar days** of the enrollment date (or of the official course start date, whichever is later) without academic penalty. The enrollment status is set to “dropped” and the course does **not** appear on the academic transcript as a failed or incomplete attempt.

### 2.2 Late Withdrawal
Withdrawals requested **after the 14-day window** are recorded as “dropped”. The course remains visible on the student’s academic record. Late withdrawals may affect:
- Scholarship eligibility calculations
- Academic progress reviews
- Future recommendations

## 3. Process
1. The student (or an authorized instructor/registrar acting on the student’s behalf) initiates a withdrawal request through the academic system or via a formal written request.
2. For withdrawals after day 14, the system requires explicit confirmation (elicitation) because the academic consequences are material.
3. Once confirmed, the enrollment status is updated to “dropped” and the student loses access to course materials and assessments for that course.

## 4. Instructor-Initiated Withdrawal
An instructor may recommend withdrawal of a student who has:
- Attendance below 50% after the midpoint of the course, **and**
- Failed to respond to at least two formal attendance warnings.

The final decision rests with the registrar. The student must be given an opportunity to respond before the status is changed.

## 5. Financial Implications
Tuition refund rules are governed by the separate Finance Policy. Academic withdrawal status does not automatically trigger a refund; the student must also satisfy the finance office’s requirements.

## 6. Re-Enrollment
A student who has withdrawn (early or late) may re-enroll in the same course in a subsequent term, subject to seat availability and any academic-probation restrictions.

## 7. Interaction with Attendance and Scholarship Policies
- Early withdrawals (within 14 days) do not generate attendance sanctions.
- Late withdrawals after attendance has already fallen below 75% still count as a dropped enrollment for scholarship maintenance reviews.
- A student on academic probation who withdraws from a required course may face additional review by the Academic Affairs Office.
